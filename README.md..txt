# 🏥 Regulatory Compliance in U.S. Healthcare Insurance Operations

This repository documents key compliance frameworks shaping healthcare insurance operations in the United States, with a focus on **CMS, HIPAA, ACA, ERISA, and COBRA**.  
It is designed as a knowledge base for professionals in **claims adjudication, benefits analysis, and compliance auditing**.

---

## 📜 Major Compliance Frameworks

### CMS Regulations
- Eligibility & Enrollment verification
- Billing & Coding standards (ICD‑10, CPT, HCPCS, NCCI)
- Coverage Determinations (LCDs, NCDs)
- Program Integrity (fraud detection, duplicate claims)
- Prepayment & Postpayment Reviews
- Documentation Standards
- Appeals & Reopenings
- Conditions of Participation (CoPs)
- Quality Programs (QAPI)

### HIPAA
- Privacy Rule → Patient control of PHI  
- Security Rule → Safeguards for ePHI  
- Breach Notification Rule → Mandatory reporting  
- Enforcement Rule → Civil penalties  
- Transactions Rule → Standardized electronic transactions  

### ACA
- Employer Mandate (50+ FTEs)  
- Essential Health Benefits  
- Consumer Protections (no lifetime/annual limits)  
- Reporting (Forms 1094‑C, 1095‑C)  

### ERISA
- Fiduciary Duties  
- Plan Documentation (SPDs, disclosures)  
- Reporting (Form 5500)  
- Enforcement mechanisms  

### COBRA
- Continuation coverage after job loss  
- Notice requirements  
- Premiums (up to 102% of plan cost)  
- Coverage duration (18–36 months)
